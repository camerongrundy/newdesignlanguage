03

Tabs

Two switching patterns, each with a job. Raised tabs partition a page; the segmented control toggles between views of the same records. Both are keyboard-driven with arrow keys.

Raised tabs — page-level partitions

Section One

The selected tab lifts onto a white surface with a soft shadow; unselected tabs are transparent and take a 66% white wash on hover. Nothing moves horizontally, so the row never reflows as you click across it.

Section Two

Each panel is a plain region. Panels are hidden with the hidden attribute rather than display:none in CSS, so assistive tech and in-page search agree with what is on screen.

A Much Longer Section Name

Selection persists per tab group. Arrow Left and Arrow Right move focus and selection; Tab moves out of the group entirely, which is the WAI-ARIA automatic-activation pattern.

Section Four

Tab labels wrap to a second line rather than scrolling or truncating — a long label has to stay legible at 360px.

Segmented control — one dataset, two views

The pressed segment takes the white surface and a two-layer shadow. Reach for this only when the options are views of the same records — if the content differs, it is a tab.

04

Breadcrumbs

The trail sits above the page title, never beside it. Every step before the last is a link; the last is the page you are on and is not clickable. Levels are separated by a single chevron.

Dashboard › Records
Dashboard › Records › REC-0001
Dashboard › Records › Northgate District › Activity

Four levels is the ceiling. A middle step whose label runs long truncates with an ellipsis and keeps its full label in a tooltip; the first and last steps never truncate. Hover underlines a link and darkens it one step.

05

A full record page as one block: title, filter chips and the table itself. Everything works — search, chips, sorting, row hover, individual and select-all checkboxes, and the bulk action bar that appears once something is selected. All data is fabricated.

07

Colors

The Foundation ramps from the 2026 Design System file. Seven hues plus neutrals, each on a four-step ramp: 900 is the true colour and the only step that carries text or an icon, 800 is a border or a chart secondary, 700 is a fill, 600 is the faintest wash. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Foundation ramps

Contrast: only the 900 step of each hue clears 4.5:1 on white — except primary/900 and tertiary/900, which are fill-and-accent colours and must never carry body text.
